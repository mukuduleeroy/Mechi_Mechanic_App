# Demo guide

1. Open Mechi, or select another vehicle under Vehicles.
2. Tap Request assistance. Select Roadside, Flat tyre and Avondale Shopping Centre. For a home visit, pick Home service and Routine service.
3. Swipe the mechanic cards or select a marker. Open Tariro Moyo’s profile and tap Get quote. For Flat tyre, the quote is USD 36.00 with USD 18 labour, USD 6 parts and USD 12 call-out.
4. Accept the quote and use the demo controls to simulate departure, arrival and repair. Return Home at any point and use Open current job to resume.
5. Open Review completion. Read the work and parts, then confirm completion and continue to payment.
6. Select EcoCash, card or Zimswitch to simulate a digital payment. Use Simulate failure on next attempt before submitting to demonstrate failure and retry. For Cash, confirm the payment choice and then simulate receipt of cash.
7. Print the receipt, submit a rating, and reopen the job and receipt from Job history.

In a debug build, Profile offers the component gallery and Reset demo data. Reset asks for confirmation and clears local changes.
